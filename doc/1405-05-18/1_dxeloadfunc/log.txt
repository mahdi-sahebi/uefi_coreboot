**1. Is it valid that `Ia32/DxeLoadFunc.c` is executed instead of the X64 version?**

**Yes, this is completely normal and correct.**

- PEI phase always runs in 32-bit mode.
- The IA32 version of `HandOffToDxeCore` is the one that decides whether to stay in 32-bit or switch to long mode.
- You entered the long-mode path because `FeaturePcdGet(PcdDxeIplSwitchToLongMode)` returned TRUE.

**2. Is the problem about `STACK_SIZE = 0`?**

**No.**

- `#define STACK_SIZE 0x20000` is correct.

  - `BaseOfStack = 0x63443000`
  - `TopOfStack  = 0x63462fe0`
- Calculation: `0x63443000 + 0x20000 - 32 = 0x63462fe0` → perfect match.
- The `//0` you wrote in the comments was just a mis-reading of the screen. The stack size is fine.

**3. Why do we see nothing after this point?**
